The loop

Every ingredient is a bad idea worth trying.

Peck up strange food, build pressure, fight the laying meter, and discover what comes out. A perfect shift can end in a museum piece. A reckless one can end in a harmless, spectacular pop.

01

Feed the experiment

Mix peppers, soap, magnets, watermelon and more. Traits combine into heavier, stranger and rarer eggs.

02

Master the pressure

Keep your balance when the needle climbs. Push your luck for a bigger egg—or hit emergency labor before the farm erupts.

03

Build the collection

Discover recipes, chase rarity stars and turn the Egg Museum into a gallery of questionable poultry science.

Pressure system

Calm hen. Terrible decisions.

The pressure gauge reacts to what you eat and how long you wait. Warning states, cracked eggs and expressive hen moods make every near-disaster readable—and funnier.

Rooster Raid

The rooster heard about your eggs.

A live farm event turns the whole yard into a chase. Dodge, protect the nest and survive the countdown while feathers—and dignity—go everywhere.

More ways to play

Keep the evidence.

Fill the Egg Museum, then use Photo Mode to pose the culprit, tune the light and capture the aftermath.

One farm. No nonsense.

A complete comedy game, not a subscription treadmill.

HENORMOUS is a single-player game with local progress, English and Turkish, accessibility settings, keyboard, controller and touch foundations, and a planned one-time lifetime full-game unlock on mobile.

No account. No behavioral ad tracking. No automatic screenshot uploads.
